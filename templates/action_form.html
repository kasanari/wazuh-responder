ADS-24 Network Operator Control Panel

Use the form below to send commands to Wazuh agents, by selecting an action and an agent from the dropdown menus. You will also need to provide a short reason for your action. Shutdown will power off the agent, and isolate will prevent it from communicating with other subnets.

The table below shows a log of your actions. The "Result" column indicates whether the command was successful or not. Commands sent to shut down or isolate agents will typically fail if the agent is already shut down. The reset button will clear the log, and should not be done by the operator.
